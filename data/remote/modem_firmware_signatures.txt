Modem-partition firmware sets: split images (.mdt + .bNN), ELF headers, signature-segment hash coverage, and signing chains.
Method: .mdt ELF headers parsed; each non-empty program segment file bNN was hashed with SHA-384 and searched inside the signature segment (the segment with flags 0x2000000). Certificates were located by DER scan of the signature segment and read with openssl.
